📲 *APP INSTALL GUIDE*
Satelkar's Logistics — It's Baked
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Install the app on your phone so it works
like a normal app — no Play Store needed!

════════════════════════════
📱 *ANDROID PHONE — Chrome Browser*
════════════════════════════

*Step 1:*
Open *Google Chrome* browser on your phone

*Step 2:*
Go to this link:
👉 https://bakery-gps.vercel.app
(for drivers)
👉 https://bakery-gps.vercel.app/driver
(direct driver page)

*Step 3:*
Wait for the page to fully load

*Step 4 — Option A (Banner appears automatically):*
If you see a popup at the bottom saying
*"Add Baked GPS to Home Screen"*
→ Tap *"Install"* or *"Add"*
→ Done! ✅

*Step 4 — Option B (No banner):*
1. Tap the *3 dots menu* ⋮
   (top right corner of Chrome)
2. Scroll down → tap *"Add to Home screen"*
   OR tap *"Install app"*
3. A popup will appear — tap *"Add"* or *"Install"*
4. Done! ✅

*Step 5:*
Find the app icon on your home screen
Tap it to open — it works like a normal app!

---

════════════════════════════
🍎 *IPHONE — Safari Browser*
════════════════════════════

⚠️ On iPhone, use *Safari* — not Chrome
   (Chrome on iPhone cannot install apps)

*Step 1:*
Open *Safari* browser on your iPhone

*Step 2:*
Go to:
👉 https://bakery-gps.vercel.app

*Step 3:*
Wait for the page to fully load

*Step 4:*
Tap the *Share button* 📤
(bottom center of the screen — box with arrow)

*Step 5:*
Scroll down in the share menu
Tap *"Add to Home Screen"* 📲

*Step 6:*
You will see the app name
Tap *"Add"* (top right corner)

*Step 7:*
Done! ✅ App icon appears on home screen

---

════════════════════════════
💻 *LAPTOP / COMPUTER — Chrome*
════════════════════════════

*Step 1:*
Open *Google Chrome* on your computer

*Step 2:*
Go to: https://bakery-gps.vercel.app

*Step 3:*
Look for the install icon in the address bar
(small computer icon 🖥 or download icon ⬇)
at the right side of the address bar

*Step 4:*
Click it → click *"Install"*

*Step 5:*
App opens as a separate window
Also added to your taskbar / desktop!

---

════════════════════════════
✅ *AFTER INSTALLING*
════════════════════════════

• App icon will appear on your home screen
• Opens without browser address bar
• Works faster than browser
• When you open app — login with your name & password

*Allow these permissions when asked:*
📍 Location — needed for GPS & delivery tracking
🔔 Notifications — needed for new order alerts

---

════════════════════════════
❓ *PROBLEM INSTALLING?*
════════════════════════════

*Android — "Install" option not showing:*
→ Clear Chrome cache:
   Settings → Chrome → Storage → Clear Cache
→ Try again

*iPhone — "Add to Home Screen" not visible:*
→ Make sure you are using Safari (not Chrome)
→ Scroll up/down in the share menu

*App not loading after install:*
→ Check internet connection
→ Delete app → reinstall using steps above
