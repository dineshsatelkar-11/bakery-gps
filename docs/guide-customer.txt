Your bakery delivery is now fully trackable.
Here's how to use our tracking page:

---

📲 *HOW TO TRACK YOUR DELIVERY*

1️⃣ Open this link in your phone browser:
   👉 https://bakery-gps.vercel.app/tracking

2️⃣ Enter your *registered mobile number*
   (the one shared with us when your shop was added)

3️⃣ Tap *"Track My Delivery"* 🔍

---

📦 *WHAT YOU WILL SEE*

✅ Your shop name & area
✅ Your assigned delivery driver name + call button
✅ Last delivery time & location (map link)
✅ Live tracking link (if driver has GPS enabled)

---

📞 *NEED HELP? — CONTACT US*

If you have any issue with your delivery:

▶ First tap *"Issue not resolved? Escalate"*
   → Connects you to our Delivery Manager

▶ If still not resolved, tap *"Escalate to Owner"*
   → Connects you directly to the company owners

  to update your details
